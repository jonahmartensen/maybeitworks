When I ask you: who do you want to be? You cannot answer ⸺ When I ask you: who do you dare to be? Your eyes start watering ⸺ I reach to your hand and there is a small mole, I stroke it. And then I say: close your eyes, hear the music, DANCE, let loose, let the world fade and find within: courage, daring, grandeur and strength.
